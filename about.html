About Me

I am a results-driven Senior CAE Engineer with 11+ years of experience in full-vehicle multibody simulation, ride comfort and handling analysis, and vehicle dynamics model validation for OEM passenger car programs — including control system integration for active chassis systems under SiL/MiL co-simulation environments.

Based in the Stuttgart region, Germany, I work across the full development cycle — from concept-phase suspension modelling in MSC ADAMS/Car and SIMPACK to closed-loop co-simulation environments coupling Simulink controllers with full-vehicle MBS plant models — and build Python tooling that automates simulation pipelines and KPI reporting for the wider engineering team.
