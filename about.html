About me

Profile

I am a Senior CAE Engineer with 11+ years of experience in full-vehicle multibody simulation (MBS), ride comfort and handling analysis, and vehicle dynamics model validation across OEM passenger car and Tier-1 supplier programmes. Based in the Stuttgart region, Germany, I work across the full development cycle — from concept-phase suspension modelling in ADAMS/Car and SIMPACK through to series maturity.

My focus is control integration for active chassis systems. I build closed-loop Software-in-the-Loop (SiL) and Model-in-the-Loop (MiL) co-simulation environments that couple Simulink controller FMUs with full-vehicle MBS models, so controllers can be validated virtually without physical prototype hardware. I also develop Python/PyQt tools for batch simulation and KPI evaluation, and I am pursuing part-time doctoral research at KU Leuven on semi-active suspension control.

Outside work, I build Python and MATLAB tools, published on GitHub, and share what I am learning on my YouTube channel. I also enjoy reading non-fiction.
